**Guardrails AI**

1. The Three Layers of Protection
Guardrails typically operate at three distinct stages of a request:  

Input Guardrails: Scan user prompts for malicious intent (jailbreaks), prompt injection, or sensitive data (PII) before the model even sees them.  

Retrieval Guardrails: In RAG (Retrieval-Augmented Generation) systems, these scan the documents retrieved from your database to ensure they don't contain "poisoned" info or secrets that shouldn't be summarized.

Output Guardrails: Check the AI's response before the user sees it to block hallucinations, toxic language, or "off-topic" business answers (e.g., ensuring a car dealership bot doesn't start giving medical advice).
